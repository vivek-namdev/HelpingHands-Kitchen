<h1 align="center">🍽️ HelpingHands Kitchen</h1>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Reducing food waste. Feeding communities.</strong>
</p>

<p align="center">
  HelpingHands Kitchen is a MERN stack platform that connects food donors
  with NGOs to help redistribute surplus edible food to communities in need.
</p>

<hr />

<h2>🌱 About the Project</h2>

<p>
  HelpingHands Kitchen creates a bridge between people and organizations
  with surplus food and NGOs that can collect and distribute it.
  The platform makes the process of donating, claiming, and coordinating
  food simple and organized.
</p>

<h2>✨ Key Features</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Donor and NGO registration</li>
  <li>Role-based authentication for Donors, NGOs, and Admins</li>
  <li>Create and manage food donations</li>
  <li>Interactive map-based pickup location selection</li>
  <li>Automatic address detection using reverse geocoding</li>
  <li>NGOs can discover and claim available donations</li>
  <li>Donation and claim status tracking</li>
  <li>Admin dashboard for monitoring the platform</li>
  <li>Email notification when a donation is claimed</li>
</ul>

<h2>🔄 How It Works</h2>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Donor → Donation → NGO Claim → Pickup → Community</strong>
</p>

<ol>
  <li>A donor registers and adds surplus food.</li>
  <li>The donation becomes available on the platform.</li>
  <li>An NGO finds and claims the donation.</li>
  <li>Pickup is coordinated using the provided location.</li>
  <li>The food reaches the community that needs it.</li>
</ol>
